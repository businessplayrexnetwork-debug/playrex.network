Help

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Playrex Network?

A gaming community platform for players, tournaments and verified organizers.

How do I join a tournament?

Choose an approved event from the Tournament Showcase and follow its registration details.

How can I become an organizer?

Sign in and submit the organizer application for Playrex team review.

Who approves organizers?

The Playrex team reviews each application and decides whether access is granted.

Can an unapproved organizer create tournaments?

No. Tournament creation is available only after approval.

When does a tournament appear?

In this demo, approving an organizer instantly creates a showcase tournament. A production backend would do this securely.

Which games are supported?

ScarFall 2.0, Mobile Legends: Bang Bang, BGMI and Free Fire MAX are currently showcased.

Can Playrex publish news?

Yes. The Admin Demo includes a simple news publishing flow.

Is Google login real here?

No. It is a simulated login for the demo. Real Google OAuth needs a configured authentication provider.

Is my approval data stored?

This demo uses your browser's localStorage. A production site should use a secure database with admin rules.
